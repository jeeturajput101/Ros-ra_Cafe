Our story

A cup, unhurried.

Roséra opened as a quiet answer to hurried coffee — twin maroon awnings, glass walls that catch the evening light, and a bar where every pour gets its own two minutes. Behind the counter, beans are roasted in small batches and milk is steamed to order, never queued.

Inside, wooden bistro chairs and potted palms turn the front room into a patio you can sit in even after dark. It's the kind of café you come to for one coffee and stay for three.

Cool pours

Built for warm afternoons

Our cold bar runs on the same beans as the hot one — slow-steeped over ice, packed to travel, and finished with the same red Roséra seal you'll find on every takeaway bag.

Order it in, or carry it out — either way, it holds its cold for the walk home.

Good to know

A few questions we get often

Yes — the fenced front patio behind the maroon awnings is open seating, shaded in the day and lit warmly after dark. It's first-come on weekdays and reservable on weekends.

Yes, free Wi-Fi throughout, and the inside room has power outlets along the window counter — it's a regular spot for weekend laptop work.

Call the counter a day ahead and we'll set aside croissants, babka, or a whole sourdough loaf before the morning batch sells out.

Two-wheeler parking sits right along the front fence; car parking is available at the Central Market square, a two-minute walk away.

Well-behaved, leashed pets are welcome on the outdoor patio. We keep a water bowl by the front gate.
